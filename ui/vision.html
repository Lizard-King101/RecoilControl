Connect your physical mouse to detect fire in your game.

Linux will ask for administrator approval to give your user read access to this mouse device until unplug/reboot. The app reads the left button without blocking the game. Other processes under your user can also read this device.

Start the countdown and switch to your game. The high tone means armed: aim, then hold left mouse to record. Release ends the take and stops assistance.

One take per arming, ending on release (30 seconds maximum). Movement ends after the selected pattern; it never loops. If already holding fire at the tone, release and press again. Screen capture stays on between takes.

Optional integration: the simulator sends fire state every 100 ms. Release ends the take; a missing heartbeat disarms output.

KDE/GNOME asks for pointer permission. No clicks or keyboard input are generated.

The lab never presses fire. Stop / disarm or Escape works while this window has focus. Capture loss or closing the lab stops the countdown and output.
